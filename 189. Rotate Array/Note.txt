There is a direct relation between left rotation and right rotation of an array.

If an array has length `n`, then:

- Left rotate by `k` is the same as right rotate by `n - k`
- Right rotate by `k` is the same as left rotate by `n - k`

This works because both rotations just move elements in opposite directions while wrapping around the array.

Example:

For array `1 2 3 4 5`:

- Left rotate by `2` gives `3 4 5 1 2`
- Right rotate by `3` also gives `3 4 5 1 2`

So, left and right rotation are complementary operations.
